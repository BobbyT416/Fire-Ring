At a Glance

Kentuck Campground has about 200 campsites, many with electric hookups, plus 27 walk-to sites and three multi-sites. The campground has a dump station and shower houses. Every campsite has a picnic table, fire ring, and parking space. 

Important Rules & Safety

🏞️ State park rules Pennsylvania state parks operate under Title 17 rules. Fire Ring should surface the applicable official rules rather than relying only on user posts.
🚣 Whitewater PFDs are required while boating on the river; helmets are required on the Lower Youghiogheny. Water levels can change river difficulty.

Things To Do

💧 Ohiopyle Falls

One of the park's signature sights. Great place to explore the Youghiogheny River gorge.

🥾 Ferncliff Trail

1.7-mile loop around Ferncliff Peninsula with river and gorge scenery.

🚲 Great Allegheny Passage

Popular rail-trail route through Ohiopyle and beyond.

💦 Cucumber Falls

A popular waterfall reached by a short, steep trail.

🌊 Meadow Run

Natural waterslides and creek area. Conditions can change quickly.

🚣 Whitewater

Boating and rafting opportunities on the Youghiogheny River.

🪨 Baughman Rock

Scenic overlook with views into the Youghiogheny River gorge.
